Verified Campus Exchange Spots

CampusCart was built to eliminate risky off-campus stranger meetings. Complete all exchanges safely at these official, high-traffic university landmarks with CCTV and security presence.

Ground Floor Foyer & Study Tables

The gold standard for textbook and notes exchanges. Plenty of natural light, security guards at the turnstiles, and study tables to inspect pages and bindings thoroughly.

🕒 Recommended Hours: Mon - Sat: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM

The CampusCart Safe Exchange Code

Always follow these 4 simple university trading principles to protect yourself and your peers:

✓
Always inspect in person before paying: Never send advance token deposits or UPI transfers to anyone before physically checking the textbook, calculator, or gadget.
✓
Stick to designated public spots: Never agree to meet at isolated parking lots or off-campus alleyways. The library or cafeteria are always within a 5-minute walk.
✓
Verify student status: Both parties can verify student ID cards upon meeting for 100% peace of mind.
✓
Use direct instant payment: Complete payment via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or cash on the spot right after inspecting and accepting the item.
