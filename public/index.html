How CampusCart Works

Buying and selling within your campus community is safe, fast, and completely commission-free.

01

List in 60 Seconds

Take a couple of photos of your used textbooks, calculator or gadgets, set an honest student-friendly price, and publish your listing.

02

Connect with Campus Peers

Interested buyers from your university contact you directly. Chat, discuss item condition, and agree on a convenient time.

03

Safe Campus Hand-Off

Meet up safely on campus—at the university library, cafeteria, or hostel gate. Inspect the item in person and complete the exchange.

Built Exclusively for University Students

Traditional marketplaces like OLX or Facebook Marketplace often come with anonymous strangers, shipping delays, scam risks, and hefty platform fees. CampusCart brings commerce right to your campus doorstep.

✓

Student Verified Profiles

Buy and sell with classmates and verified students from your own university community.

✓

No Shipping Fees or Waiting

Pick up items within minutes between classes or right outside your hostel block.

✓

Eco-Friendly Circular Economy

Keep educational resources in active circulation, cutting costs and waste semester after semester.

Campus Safety Code

  • 📍 Always meet in well-lit public campus spaces (e.g. Library foyer, Student Center).
  • 🔍 Inspect items thoroughly in person before concluding any payment or exchange.
  • 💳 Use direct UPI/cash upon handover—never send advance deposits to strangers.
